## The angle (provisional, pick after a segment is chosen)

1. For small teams who plan on their phone, {app} makes the whole rota editable on a phone, with a change log that shows who changed what. Evidence: H3, H7: 0 verified reviews. **Weak.**
2. For shops and restaurants where clock-ins get forgotten, {app} flags missing clock-outs and fixes them in one tap. Evidence: H8: 0 verified. **Weak.**
3. For a chosen niche (care, hospitality, cleaning) with its own rules built in. Evidence: none yet. **Strong if a segment is chosen, because depth in one segment beats breadth.**

**Recommendation: option 3**, with the change log as a trust feature. Do not name the original in the app name, ads or store listing.
